What I'd hand off

Candlelight wipe as the family, ledger slide for the light moves. The wipe is the only one of the four that is already true of this product: every space in The Scaffold is lit by one amber source, so moving the light between rooms explains the navigation instead of decorating it. It also flexes honestly, since a full sweep with darkness trailing reads as a big move, while the same sweep shortened to a brightness pass over a held layout reads as a tab change.

Into and out of Wick's room, let the wipe start at his lamp and leave with it. Going in, the light arrives from the right and warms the room by about 8% past the Command Center's level; coming out, it drains left and the temperature drops back. That single asymmetry is what makes his room feel like a different place rather than another screen.

Tradeoffs worth knowing: the curtain is the most theatrical and the least reusable, and at anything under about 900ms it reads as a glitch rather than drapery, so I would keep it for a first-load or lock-screen moment only. The ledger flip is the most on-theme for campaign history but costs a full second at full size and needs a 3D transform on a whole screen, so it is best reserved for moving through records, previous month, next month, rather than between rooms. Fog is the easiest to build and the most forgiving on slow machines, but it says the least about where you are going.
